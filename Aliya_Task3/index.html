HOW IT WORKS

Conversion formulas

°C → °F

Multiply Celsius by 9/5, then add 32.

(°C × 9/5) + 32
°F → °C

Subtract 32 from Fahrenheit, then multiply by 5/9.

(°F − 32) × 5/9
°C ↔ K

Add 273.15 to convert Celsius to Kelvin.

K = °C + 273.15

Did you know? Absolute zero is 0 K, −273.15°C, or −459.67°F. Temperatures below absolute zero are not accepted.
